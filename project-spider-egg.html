Mission

Spider Egg emphasizes stealth and survivability through quiet electric propulsion, multi-layered PACE communications, and minimal time exposed at the surface. Its open, platform-agnostic architecture lets it integrate with submarines, surface combatants, and large UUVs, delivering anything from a precise mission kill to a coordinated hard kill from one system.

Capabilities

  • Quiet electric propulsion: a high-energy-density lithium-ion battery and a low-noise electric motor keep the acoustic signature minimal at speeds up to 30 knots
  • Mission-flexible lower payload bay: holds up to three Spiders, sensor pods, or smaller specialized UUVs
  • Versatile upper payload deck: FPV drones, electronic warfare modules, and buoyant relay sensors
  • Thermal imaging & PACE comms: a mast raises thermal/EO sensors plus SATCOM, Iridium, HF, and VHF links when surfaced
  • Autonomous distributed strikes: directs Spiders individually or couples them to concentrate effects on priority threats
  • Platform-agnostic integration: works with submarine combat systems, surface combatant networks, and large UUVs

The Spider

Each Spider is a compact kinetic effector: 1.5 m long, with electric propulsion, agile fin and rudder control surfaces, and onboard mesh networking for cooperative targeting. It is smaller than a standard Mk 54 torpedo and can launch from LWT tubes, large UUVs, beach-launched assets, aerial drones, or a Spider Egg capsule.
