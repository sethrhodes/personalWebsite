Light Up Puerto Rico

A community initiative that paired a solar flashlight I designed and manufactured with hands-on renewable energy workshops in Puerto Rican schools. In partnership with the American Red Cross of Puerto Rico, we distributed 1,000 solar flashlights for disaster relief.

The Problem

When storms knock out the grid in Puerto Rico, communities can go without power for long stretches. A reliable light that recharges from the sun is a simple but important tool for disaster preparedness, and understanding how it works makes renewable energy tangible for the next generation.

The Flashlight

I designed and manufactured a mini solar flashlight that is compact and recharged by sunlight, so it keeps working when the power doesn't.

Workshops

I led solar technology workshops for more than 300 students across Puerto Rican schools. Students assembled solar-powered circuits hands-on, connecting what they built to the basics of renewable energy and engineering.

Distribution

Working with the American Red Cross of Puerto Rico, we distributed 1,000 solar flashlights to support disaster relief efforts.
