An affordable reaction-time trainer that makes motorsports preparation accessible. It grew out of R&D on the neuroscience of motorsports, and it replaces expensive traditional training with a compact steering-wheel and pedal rig. In testing, it raised users' reaction speeds by 6% on average.

The Problem

Reaction time matters in motorsports, but the tools drivers use to train it are expensive and hard to access. I wanted a low-cost device that lets more people practice the same reflexes.

The Design

  • Steering-wheel controller: a 3D-printed wheel with a built-in LCD that prompts the driver
  • Pedals: hinged throttle and brake pedals mounted on a low base
  • Electronics: an Arduino, a 16x2 LCD, buttons, and LEDs, prototyped on a breadboard and then built into the enclosure
  • Training loop: the LCD cues the driver (“Press Start To Begin,” “PUNCH IT!”) and times their response

Results

In user testing, people who trained with the tool improved their reaction speed by 6% on average.
